# UI Simplification & Layout Optimization Prompt

Redesign the University of Layyah AI Assistant homepage to be cleaner, more focused, and chatbot-centric.

## Navigation Bar

Keep only the following menu items:

* Home
* AI Chat
* Admin Dashboard

Remove completely:

* Programs
* Fee Structure
* Faculty
* Events
* FAQs

The chatbot itself will handle all queries related to programs, fees, faculty, events, admissions, campus information, and student life.

## Hero Section

Keep the modern green and orange University of Layyah branding.

Main Heading:

"Your Smart Campus Companion"

Subheading:

"Ask anything about admissions, programs, faculty, campus information, student life, events, fees, and university policies through one intelligent AI assistant."

## Hero Buttons

Keep only one primary button:

"Chat with AI"

Remove:

* Explore Programs

The chatbot should be the central focus of the entire platform.

## Remove Statistics Section

Delete completely:

* Programs Offered
* Faculty Members
* Students Enrolled
* Years of Excellence

The homepage should not look like a university marketing website.

## Remove Vision & Mission Section

Delete completely:

* Our Vision
* Our Mission
* HEC Recognized
* Distinctive Learning

These details are unnecessary for the AI assistant platform.

## Homepage Structure

The homepage should contain only:

1. Navigation Bar
2. Hero Section
3. AI Chat Preview Section
4. Features Section
5. Footer

Nothing else.

## AI Chat Preview Section

Add a modern chatbot preview card showing sample conversations:

User:
"What BS programs are available?"

AI:
"The University of Layyah offers BS Computer Science, BS Information Technology, BS Mathematics, and other programs."

User:
"What is the admission fee?"

AI:
"Here is the latest fee structure information..."

Include:

* Chat bubble design
* Typing animation
* Modern glassmorphism card

## Features Section

Display only 4 feature cards:

### Admissions & Programs

Get information about admissions, eligibility, scholarships, and fee structures.

### Campus Information

Find faculty directories, departments, locations, and office contacts.

### Student Life

Learn about exam rules, attendance policies, and student activities.

### AI Assistant

Receive instant answers 24/7 through natural conversation.

## Footer

Simplify the footer.

Remove completely:

* University logo
* University name
* Address block
* Contact card
* Ask the AI button inside footer

Footer should contain only:

© 2026 University of Layyah. All Rights Reserved.

Privacy Policy | Terms of Service

Centered and minimal.

## Design Guidelines

* Maintain University of Layyah green and orange theme.
* Modern and premium UI.
* Responsive design.
* Smooth animations.
* Clean spacing.
* Focus entirely on the AI chatbot experience.
* The website should feel like an AI-powered university assistant, not a traditional university information portal.

## Important Rule

All university information (Programs, Admissions, Fee Structure, Faculty, Events, Campus Information, Student Life) must be accessible only through the AI Chat Assistant.

Do not create separate pages or navigation links for these sections.

# University of Layyah AI Assistant – Complete Project Generation Prompt



Build a modern, intelligent, production-ready web-based AI Chatbot called "UL AI Assistant" for the University of Layyah.

The chatbot must serve as a single unified AI assistant that can answer all university-related queries through one conversational interface. Do not create separate systems for Admissions, Campus Information, and Student Life. Everything must be integrated into a single AI chatbot experience where users simply ask questions in natural language and receive accurate answers instantly.

## Core Objective

Create an AI-powered university assistant capable of helping:

* Prospective Students
* Current Students
* Parents
* Faculty Visitors
* Administrative Visitors

The chatbot should understand user intent and provide accurate responses related to admissions, academic programs, campus information, faculty directories, student life, university regulations, and campus events.

## Knowledge Areas

### Admissions & Programs

The AI chatbot must answer questions regarding:

* BS Degree Programs
* Eligibility Criteria
* Admission Requirements
* Fee Structures
* Scholarships
* Merit Information
* Required Documents
* Application Process
* Admission Deadlines

Example Questions:

* What BS programs are offered at University of Layyah?
* What is the eligibility for BS Information Technology?
* What documents are required for admission?
* What is the fee structure?

### Campus Information

The chatbot must provide:

* Faculty Directory
* Department Information
* Office Locations
* Building Locations
* Desk Extensions
* Contact Information
* Administrative Offices
* Campus Facilities

Example Questions:

* Where is the Computer Science Department?
* Who is the Head of the IT Department?
* Give me the contact information of the Admission Office.
* Where is the Registrar Office located?

### Student Life

The chatbot must answer:

* Examination Guidelines
* Attendance Rules
* Academic Policies
* Student Regulations
* Campus Events
* Workshops
* Seminars
* Student Societies
* General Student Support Information

Example Questions:

* What is the minimum attendance requirement?
* What are the examination rules?
* Tell me about upcoming events.
* What student societies are available?

## AI Features

The chatbot must:

* Understand natural language questions.
* Maintain conversation history.
* Handle follow-up questions.
* Provide contextual answers.
* Generate human-like responses.
* Suggest related information when relevant.
* Support English and Urdu languages.
* Provide quick and accurate responses.
